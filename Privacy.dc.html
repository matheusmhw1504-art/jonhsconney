Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

The short version: this website doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number, doesn't set cookies of its own, and doesn't run analytics or advertising trackers. We don't sell or share your personal information.

Who we are

This website belongs to John's Famous Coney Island & Restaurant ("John's", "we", "us"), 5275 Corunna Rd, Flint, Michigan 48532. This policy explains what happens to information when you visit this site.

Information we collect

We don't collect personal information through this website. There are no forms, accounts, newsletter sign-ups, online orders or payments on this site.

If you call us, message us on Facebook or visit the restaurant, we only use what you tell us to answer you or serve you.

Information collected automatically

  • Hosting. Like every website, the company that hosts this site receives basic technical data when your browser loads a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't use it to identify you.
  • Map. The map on our home page is a drawing, not an embedded Google map. The "Show the map" and "Get directions" buttons open Google Maps in a new tab. Once you are on Google Maps, Google receives your IP address and may set cookies, as described in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Fonts. The lettering on this site is loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver it, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn't set cookies of its own and doesn't use analytics, advertising pixels or other tracking tools. If you follow a link to Google Maps or Facebook, those sites may set their own cookies. Because we don't track visitors, there is nothing on our side for a browser's "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control signal to turn off.

Links to other sites

Buttons for directions, reviews and our Facebook page open Google Maps and Facebook. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we don't control what they collect.

Children

This website isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, including California, you may have the right to ask what personal information a business holds about you, to ask that it be deleted, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Since this website doesn't collect or sell personal information, there is usually nothing on file from your visit. If you have a question, contact us below and we'll answer it.

Michigan residents

We follow applicable Michigan and U.S. federal law, including the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If there is ever a security incident affecting personal information we hold, we will notify affected people as required by Michigan's Identity Theft Protection Act.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this website handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
