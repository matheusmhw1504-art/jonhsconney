Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 6, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, magnification or other assistive technology.

Our goal

We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we've done

  • Text and buttons meet WCAG color contrast ratios.
  • Every part of the site works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Headings, lists and landmarks are marked up so screen readers can navigate them.
  • Images have text descriptions, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • The rating breakdown is described in text for screen readers.
  • Text can be enlarged up to 200% without losing content, and the layout adapts to small screens.
  • Animations are turned off for visitors who turn on "reduce motion" in their device settings.
  • A "Pause animations" button, fixed to the corner of the screen, stops every moving or looping element on the page.
  • No content flashes more than three times per second.
  • A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing keyboard users reach.
  • Links that open a new tab say so to screen readers.

Known limitations

  • The menu on this website is a selection of our dishes, not the full menu. Call us and we'll read you the full menu and prices.
  • The links to Google Maps and Facebook go to sites we don't control. Their accessibility depends on those companies. Our address is always written out next to the map.
  • The photos in the "Inside John's" section are illustrations for now and are hidden from screen readers.

Need help or found a problem?

If any part of this website is hard to use, please let us know and we'll help you get the information another way. Call (810) 732-5230 or tell a manager at the restaurant. Please tell us which page you were on and what happened.
